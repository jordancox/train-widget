Treni

A Live Activity for one commute by train in Rome. On weekday mornings and evenings it turns up on the lock screen by itself, counts down to when to leave, and follows the train live until it's gone.

On your lock screen, by itself

Every weekday at the times you set, with the next train home or to work. Nothing to open, nothing to tap.

Tells you when to leave

First it counts down to setting off, your walk to the platform included. Then to the train itself.

In the Dynamic Island

A glance away in any app. Touch and hold it for the platform and the train after.

On your wrist too

The same countdown in the Smart Stack on Apple Watch. No Watch app needed.

Every train, live

All the trains that stop at both stations, from the railway's live data: platform, delay, cancellations.

Set it once

Your two stations, the walk to each platform, and when it should start by itself.

Built for one commute, Roma Tuscolana ⇄ Muratella, on iOS 18. Live data: Viaggiatreno (RFI). A small server at home starts it and pushes every change.
