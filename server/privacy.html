Treni privacy

Treni is a small personal app for catching the train in Rome, shared with a few colleagues through TestFlight. It is not on the App Store.

What the server keeps about your phone

A random id the app makes when you first open it; the push token Apple gives the app, so the server can put the Live Activity on your lock screen at your times; the stations, walk times, times and days off you set; and what the Live Activity is showing now. No name, email, location, contacts, tracking or analytics.

Where it goes

It stays on one private server in Rome and is used only to run your Live Activity. Nothing is sold or shared. Train times come from Viaggiatreno, the Italian railway's public feed: the server asks it about stations and trains, never about you. Apple delivers the pushes to your phone.

Deleting it

Delete the app and the server forgets your phone within about a month (once Apple stops accepting its push token), or after four months without use. Newer builds also keep the random id in the phone's keychain, which outlives the app, so if you install it again within that time your settings come back. To have it removed sooner, say so through TestFlight's "Send Beta Feedback".
